Owned materials

Every material and item used by the supported recipes. Enter how many you own; quantities are saved in this browser and are used by the Planner. “Net if sold” uses the prices of the selected server (fetch them in the Planner tab), minus the market sale fee and the listing registration fee. The registration fee is charged on the total listing price: for 10 items it is 10 × price per item × 2%. It is paid when you list, so it is lost if the listing doesn’t sell. Fees are calculated for selling everything you own as one listing per item type.

Craft vs buy: Artisan’s Splendent items

Expected cost of crafting every Artisan’s Splendent item from scratch, compared with its current market price on your selected server, grouped like aion2.exchange into Weapons, Armors and Jewelry. The numbers come from the crafting model published by aion2.exchange (/api/crafts), which accounts for the 25% combo chance across the lower tiers; this planner does not recompute them. Field meanings are inferred from the field names because the API is not documented.

“Craft cost” is the API’s cost field and “If inputs bought at market” is its cost_market. “Margin” is the market price minus the craft cost, as a percentage of the craft cost: positive means crafting is cheaper, negative means buying is cheaper. “Profit after fees” assumes you craft the item and sell it at the current market price: market price minus the sale fee and the registration fee, minus the craft cost. The fee percentages are the same ones used in the Owned materials tab. It assumes the item sells at that price; a lower sale price or an unsold listing (the registration fee is still lost) would reduce it. The model assumes the mastery level and carry time shown above, so your own results can differ. “Expected crafts per tier” lists the API’s attempts values in the order returned. Items with no market listing cannot be compared. Your owned quantity is shown next to an item only when its name matches an item in your Owned materials list exactly.
