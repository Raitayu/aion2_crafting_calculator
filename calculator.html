Owned materials

Every material and item used by the supported recipes. Enter how many you own; quantities are saved in this browser and are used by the Planner. “Net if sold” uses the prices of the selected server (fetch them in the Planner tab), minus the market sale fee and the listing registration fee. The registration fee is charged on the total listing price: for 10 items it is 10 × price per item × 2%. It is paid when you list, so it is lost if the listing doesn’t sell. Fees are calculated for selling everything you own as one listing per item type.

Cooking: is it worth crafting food to sell?

For every cooking recipe: the cost of buying all ingredients (market ingredients at their current lowest listing on your selected server, vendor ingredients at the fixed vendor price), against what the finished food sells for on the Market after fees. Profit is per craft, so a recipe that needs many ingredients is compared fairly with one that needs few.

Vendor ingredients (Kina)

Sold by the Cooking Merchant, bought bound, never on the Market. Prices come from your screenshot; edit them here if the vendor changes them.

How the numbers work: “Items per craft” is 1 plus the combo chance, because a combo gives 2 of the dish (assumed from the “x2 COMBO” icon in the crafting window; set the chance to 0 to ignore it). “Net per item” is the market price minus the sale fee and the registration fee. “Profit per craft” = items per craft × net per item − ingredient cost. Failed crafts (the “if hit during crafting” warning) are not modelled. Prices are single lowest listings: buying many ingredients, or selling many dishes, will move the price. Only the last column of listings tells you how deep the market is.

Soulbind reroll calculator

Pick the item slot, the stat you want, and the stats currently on your lines. Rerolling one line draws a new stat from the slot’s pool, excluding every stat currently on any line (including the line you are rerolling). The chance of the wanted stat is therefore its weight divided by the pool’s total weight minus the weights of all stats now on your lines. Stats with high weights sitting on your lines make the wanted stat more likely, so keep them. Each reroll costs one Sync Stone or one fodder item and has no other price.

Line 4, Line 5 and Soulfuse are optional. Click a padlock to leave that line untouched.

Reroll cost

One reroll uses one Sync Stone or one Unique gear piece as fodder. Sync Stones are bound to your character and can't be sold, so they are used first and count as free. The fodder price is the average of the 10 cheapest Unique pieces on the market (each item's lowest listing), or any price you type in.

Craft vs buy: Artisan’s Splendent items

Expected cost of crafting every Artisan’s Splendent item from scratch, compared with its current market price on your selected server, grouped like aion2.exchange into Weapons, Armors and Jewelry. The numbers come from the crafting model published by aion2.exchange (/api/crafts), which accounts for the 25% combo chance across the lower tiers; this planner does not recompute them. Field meanings are inferred from the field names because the API is not documented.

“Craft cost” is the API’s cost field and “If inputs bought at market” is its cost_market. “Margin” is the market price minus the craft cost, as a percentage of the craft cost: positive means crafting is cheaper, negative means buying is cheaper. “Profit after fees” assumes you craft the item and sell it at the current market price: market price minus the sale fee and the registration fee, minus the craft cost. The fee percentages are the same ones used in the Owned materials tab. It assumes the item sells at that price; a lower sale price or an unsold listing (the registration fee is still lost) would reduce it. The model assumes the mastery level and carry time shown above, so your own results can differ. “Expected crafts per tier” lists the API’s attempts values in the order returned. Items with no market listing cannot be compared. Your owned quantity is shown next to an item only when its name matches an item in your Owned materials list exactly.
